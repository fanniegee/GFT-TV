Discover the Dramatic Arts

From traditional storytelling to contemporary theatre and performance.

Our vision: The Drama Lab will grow into a platform celebrating performers, playwrights, directors, theatre groups and dramatic traditions from around the world. Features will be introduced step by step, with respect for performers' rights and permission for published scripts, photographs and recordings.

A Home for Every Stage

The Drama Lab is designed to welcome school productions, community theatre, professional companies, independent performers and cultural groups. It will celebrate different languages, traditions and forms of dramatic expression.

What We Can Build

  • Performer and theatre-group profiles
  • Play and production showcases
  • Audition and casting announcements
  • Drama lessons and acting resources
  • Interviews and behind-the-scenes features
  • Approved performance videos and event listings
