A world of traditions

Many paths, histories and communities

Religion and spirituality have shaped art, music, philosophy, community life, architecture and important moments in human history. This channel offers an introductory guide to traditions and the people who practise them.

Our guiding principle
Present traditions fairly and respectfully. Communities are diverse, and no single description represents every believer. Learning about a tradition does not require adopting its beliefs.
